Mohamed El Alami

About

I am a mathematician. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at Stony Brook University under the supervision of Mark McLean. I also did a postdoc at the University of Edinburgh under the supervision of Nick Sheridan. My research interest lies somewhere between symplectic and algebraic geometry. More specifically, I am interested in the applications of holomorphic curve theory to the study of symplectic topology and mirror symmetry.
